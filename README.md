# Nagarयोजना

> **Hamro Nagar, Aafno Sarkar: Ward's Tasks, On Your Screen.**  
> *हाम्रो नगर, आफ्नो सरकार: वडाका काम, आफ्नै स्क्रिनमा।*

**Nagarयोजना** is an open civic infrastructure monitoring and public transparency platform created for local municipal governance in Nepal, currently configured for **Biratnagar Metropolitan City (विराटनगर महानगरपालिका)**. 

The platform bridges the information gap between municipal authorities and local citizens by providing verified, real-time visibility into ward-level public works, allocated budgets, designated contractors, and construction milestones.

---

## Overview

In local governance, civic accountability begins with accessible information. **Nagarयोजना** serves as a centralized digital oversight portal where residents, community consumer committees (*Upabhokta Samiti*), and municipal engineers can track the lifecycle of neighborhood development projects.

Whether monitoring drainage upgrades, drinking water pipelines, or community health facilities, citizens have access to verified data directly from their web browser.

---

## Key Features

- **Ward-Level Infrastructure Oversight**  
  Filter and explore ongoing and completed public works across all wards or drill down into specific neighborhood projects.

- **Transparent Budget & Contractor Tracking**  
  Review verified municipal budget allocations, executing agencies, and assigned contractors for each project.

- **Milestone & Progress Tracking**  
  Real-time completion percentage metrics and progress bars with clear status indicators (**Active**, **Delayed**, **Completed**).

- **Citizen Grievance Redressal**  
  A built-in reporting channel allowing citizens to file grievances regarding construction delays, material quality, public safety, or transparency discrepancies directly to the Ward Public Oversight Committee.

- **Bilingual Interface (English & नेपाली)**  
  Full bilingual localization supporting seamless toggling between English and authentic Nepali (*नेपाली*) civic terminology and Devanagari numerals.

- **Municipal Staff Portal**  
  Secure authentication access reserved for municipal engineers and ward oversight officers.

---

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express REST API
- **Design Philosophy**: Minimalist civic design, responsive layout, full-screen viewport optimization, and high-contrast accessibility.
